Our Story

Baked with intention, shared with love.

Crumble & Co. began in 2019 in a small Dublin kitchen, when founder Maeve Callahan started baking cookies for her neighbours every Sunday. Word spread faster than the butter melted — and within a year, what started as a hobby had grown into a weekly market stall.

We believe great cookies deserve great ingredients. Every batch uses free-range eggs from a local farm, high-quality European butter, and chocolate sourced from ethical growers. We never use artificial flavours or preservatives.

Today we bake from our little shop on Wicklow Street, still by hand, still in small batches — because we think you can taste the difference.
